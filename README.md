# Jarvis v4 (Android, Kotlin)
Open in Android Studio, sync, Run. In the app do steps 1-5 once, optionally paste an Anthropic API key, tap Start.

Say "Jarvis ..." then:
 pick up / answer (while ringing no wake word needed) | reject / hang up | call <contact>
 message <contact> saying <text> (WhatsApp) | open <app> | search <query> | type <text> | read screen
 tap <text> (exact match, then the AI picks the element) | next reel | previous reel | swipe left/right
 go back | go home | recent apps | take screenshot | lock screen | open notifications
 set alarm for 7 30 am | timer for 5 minutes | remind me to <x> in 10 minutes | add task <x> | my tasks
 volume up/down | flashlight on/off | what time is it. Anything else goes to the AI brain.

STOP: "stop" (pause) | "Jarvis emergency stop" (kill) | volume up+down together | notification STOP | red button
 | Settings > Accessibility > Jarvis > off.
Safety: never operates payment/banking apps, never types into password fields, 30 actions/min cap,
AI chains max 15 steps, AI commands can't trigger more AI calls.
